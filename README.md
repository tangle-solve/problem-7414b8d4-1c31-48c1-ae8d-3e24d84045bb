### CLIM-000227: Equilibrium climate sensitivity without shared-evidence bias
- **Subfield:** Radiative forcing, clouds, and climate sensitivity
- **Open:** Constrain equilibrium warming from doubled CO₂ while correctly accounting for dependencies among historical, process, paleoclimate, and model evidence. Uncertainty bounds must survive plausible alternative aerosol histories, cloud feedbacks, and priors.
- **Status:** Quantitative uncertainty dates to the 1979 Charney assessment; IPCC AR6 narrowed the likely range to 2.5–4°C in 2021.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Determines the emissions and concentrations compatible with temperature targets.
- **Key works:** Sherwood et al. (2020), [“An Assessment of Earth’s Climate Sensitivity Using Multiple Lines of Evidence”](https://doi.org/10.1029/2019RG000678); Forster et al. (2021), [IPCC AR6, Chapter 7](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/chapter/chapter-7/).
- **Related:** Bayesian evidence synthesis with dependent studies; inverse-problem identifiability.
- **Aliases:** ECS constraint problem.
